FIFO Reservation Queue Operational Model
Open Live Reservation Queues

1. Enqueue (Tail Insertion)

When a borrower requests a checked-out book, a new node is appended to the tail pointer in O(1) time.

2. Dequeue (Head Removal)

When a copy is returned, the front node is dequeued in O(1) time, fulfilling the reservation for the earliest requester.

3. Strict Fairness

Guarantees no starvation or arbitrary preference; reservations are processed strictly in first-come, first-served sequence.
